Premier HomewareObjective: sales · Google + Meta Google · Meta · Shopify Completed 10.5xMeta ROAS

Keeping a homeware store growing on two platforms

Performance MaxSearchMeta AdsShopify reporting

The challenge

Premier Homeware sells through Shopify and advertises on Google and Meta. The job was to keep sales growing month on month without the Meta ads going stale, or Google spend leaking into campaigns that don't sell.

What I did

I treated the budget like an investment portfolio. Performance Max was converting, so it stayed the main Google campaign. When Search spent three days without a single sale, I flagged it on day three, before it could drain more budget. On Meta, frequency was creeping toward my 3.5 limit, so I rotated in new creative before performance dropped, and cut frequency in half.

How I report

Every month I pulled Google, Meta and Shopify into one snapshot for the client: pacing, ROAS, cost per conversion, orders and average order value, with the wins and concerns spelt out.

TikTok to Instagram growthObjective: traffic · TikTok to Instagram TikTok · Instagram Completed 60%+Instagram growth

Building an audience before asking for a sale

TikTok AdsInstagramAwarenessSocial growth

The challenge

Some clients, including Brands Megastore, came in with almost no Instagram following. Sending cold TikTok traffic straight to a website asks strangers to buy from a brand they've never heard of.

The idea

I made Instagram the landing page. TikTok traffic ads sent people to the brand's Instagram profile, where they could scroll, follow and get to know the brand first. Those Instagram engagers then became a warm audience to retarget on Meta, usually at a lower cost per sale than cold traffic.

The result

Instagram followers grew by more than 60%, engagement rose alongside, and the brands had a real audience to sell to.

All clients trackerObjective: catch problems early Google · Meta · TikTok Completed 12accounts checked daily

A system that catches problems early

ReportingGoogle AdsMeta AdsTikTok Ads

The challenge

Across a dozen client accounts on three platforms, a small problem can run for days before anyone notices, and that costs clients money.

What I built

A tracker with a tab per client and quick daily checks per platform. Anything past a threshold gets flagged and sent to the account lead the same day, with a screenshot. Flags roll up into weekly and monthly summaries that become next month's optimisation priorities.

Also built

A searchable Google and Meta metrics guide (what each metric means, when to look at it and how it's calculated), plus Shopify-synced Meta catalogues for catalogue sales ads.
